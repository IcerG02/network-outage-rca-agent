# Severe weather

Storms cause outages spread over several OLTs in the same region, often partial: some ports of many OLTs rather than every port of one. Strong wind gusts above 60 km/h bring down trees onto aerial cable, and lightning damages equipment and power supplies.

Correlate the outage window with precipitation, gusts and lightning strikes for the region. Prioritize repairs by number of customers affected and expect multiple simultaneous field jobs.
