# Fiber cut on a distribution segment

When all ONUs of a single PON port go offline at the same time while the other ports of the same OLT stay up, the most likely cause is a break in the feeder or distribution fiber of that port, or a failed splitter.

Check the optical power history of the port: a drop to no signal at once points to a cut, a gradual loss points to a degraded connector or splice.

Dispatch a field team with an OTDR to locate the break from the OLT side. Typical repair time for an aerial cut is two to four hours.
