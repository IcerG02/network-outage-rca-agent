# Power outage at the OLT site

If every port of one OLT drops at the same moment, check the site power first: mains failure, a tripped breaker or exhausted batteries. Reports of a power cut in the neighborhood make this the leading hypothesis.

Confirm with the utility outage map and the site's battery alarms. Service normally returns by itself when mains power is restored; if the outage is long, send a portable generator.
